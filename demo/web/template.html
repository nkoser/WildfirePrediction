About this demo

One forecast gives every pixel a full risk curve: the probability of its first fire within any number of days after the forecast date. The map reads that curve at the lead time you choose; the panel shows the whole curve for one location.

Reading the map

Colors give the probability of a first fire in the pixel within the lead time. Black pixels had a fire in the 30 days before the forecast date, which the model saw. Blue pixels burned after the forecast date, within the lead time: what the forecast is checked against.
